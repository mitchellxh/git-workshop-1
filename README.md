## Activity 3

### 1. Star this repo

Click the **Star** button near the top-right of this page. This bookmarks the repo to your profile. You can find your starred repos under your profile picture > **Your stars**.

### 2. Fork this repo

Click **Fork** (top-right), then **Create fork** on the next page. This creates a copy of the repo under your account. You'll be redirected to your copy — note the URL now shows your username.

### 3. Delete your fork

Go to your fork, click **Settings**, scroll to the bottom (**Danger Zone**), and click **Delete this repository**. Confirm by typing the repo name.

Deleting your fork does not affect this repo.
